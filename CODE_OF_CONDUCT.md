# Code of Conduct

NexusFlow is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Participants should communicate constructively, respect differing perspectives, accept feedback gracefully, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's available private contact channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
